01. Problem

Care is hard to navigate when information is fragmented.

People often begin with a vague symptom, an unfamiliar medicine name, or uncertainty about where to go. Language, distance, and disconnected services can delay an appropriate care decision.

User need

Clearer first steps

People need understandable guidance before they can make a hospital or doctor decision.

Access gap

Bangla first support

Health navigation needs to work with informal Bangla and mixed language input.

Safety gap

Urgency before information

Potential emergency signals must be recognized before a long general conversation begins.

02. Solution and product

One care navigation layer across essential health journeys.

SasthoSetu combines symptom triage, hospital and doctor discovery, medicine information, appointments, consent controlled records, and safety focused AI guidance.

05. Roadmap and scale

Build trust through verified data, evaluation, and responsible rollout.

Short term

Data quality and pilot feedback

Expand verified local directories, review safety scenarios, and test journeys with target users and health workers.

Mid term

Partner connected care

Add verified provider integrations, stronger appointment pathways, notifications, and operational monitoring.

Long term

Privacy preserving scale

Improve localized models and retrieval quality using consent aware, properly governed, and auditable data practices.

PrincipleHow it scales responsibly
PerformanceCache safe aggregate views, lazy load heavy content, and keep clinical data out of public pages.
ReliabilityReadiness checks, telemetry, audit events, fallback paths, and admin operations visibility.
PrivacyConsent controls, role based access, minimum necessary data use, and no personal data in public analytics.
